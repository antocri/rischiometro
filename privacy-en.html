Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · The Italian version prevails in case of differences.

This policy explains what data the Rischiometro iPhone app processes, why and for how long, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In short: we do not ask for your name, email or phone number, we do not use ads or tracking tools, and we do not sell data.

1. Controller

Antonio Cristiano · Email: anto.cri84@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Anonymous account identifier (a random code), to link your cars and settings to you without knowing who you are.
  • Parked car location, the name you give it and the parking time, to check hazards where the car is.
  • Current location, rounded to about 100 m, only if you enable "Alert me where I am". Each new position replaces the previous one: no history is kept.
  • Device push tokens (notifications and Lock Screen countdown), to send you alerts.
  • Reports and votes (hazard type, location and time, hail size, damage yes/no), shown to other users without any data identifying you.
  • History of alerts received, with the car location and hazard data at that time, to show your history and, on request, create the insurance PDF summary.
  • Settings, car sharing links and invite codes, and a widget code (stored only as an encrypted fingerprint).

We do not collect contacts, photos, health data, payment data or your movement history.

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time: current location and the iOS location and notification permissions.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): service security, abuse prevention and monitoring.

4. Retention

  • Current location: until the next position; deleted immediately when you turn off "Alert me where I am".
  • Car location: until you change it or delete the car.
  • Alert history and insurance summaries: 12 months.
  • Reports and votes: 30 days after the report expires.
  • Push tokens: deleted after 6 months of inactivity (3 months for the countdown).
  • Invite codes: 48 hours. Unused accounts: deleted after 12 months of inactivity.

5. Processors

  • Supabase (database and server), with data stored in a data centre in the European Union. Supabase privacy.
  • Apple: push notifications, weather data (WeatherKit), maps and parking search (Apple Maps), in-app purchases. Apple privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass and Apple WeatherKit only receive the coordinates of areas of about 5 km from our server, not linked to any user.

Some Apple services may process data outside the EU under GDPR safeguards (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses). In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple: we never receive payment data.

6. What we do not do

We do not sell or share data for advertising or marketing, we do not use ads, profiling or tracking tools, and we do not make automated decisions with legal effects.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing (Arts. 15-22 GDPR), and withdraw consent at any time. In the app, under Settings › Privacy and my data, you can export a copy of your data or delete your account and data immediately. You can also email anto.cri84@gmail.com and lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

8. Children

Rischiometro is not intended for children under 14.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, notify you in the app.
